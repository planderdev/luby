# Luby AI Guide — Getting Started

> Audience: Everyone using Luby AI for the first time · 최종 갱신 2026-08-23 · https://luby.im

## 1. What is Luby AI
Luby AI is a **global gifting/campaign marketing platform**. Advertisers (brands, small businesses, agencies) launch campaigns, creators apply, AI helps with matching and review, and everything from content publishing to point settlement is handled in one place.

- **Advertisers**: Just enter a one-line business description and AI drafts the entire campaign → operations team review → recruitment → selection → content review → performance report
- **Creators**: Register channel → get personalized campaign recommendations and instant alerts → apply → experience & create content → earn points → cash out to bank account starting at 10,000P
- **Global**: 12 markets including Korea, Japan, Taiwan, Southeast Asia, and the US; 8 channels including Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Blog, Xiaohongshu, and Lemon8
- **Public pages**: Campaign pages viewable without login (`luby.im/c/…`), creator profiles (`luby.im/p/…`), and the open-recruitment directory (`luby.im/c`)
