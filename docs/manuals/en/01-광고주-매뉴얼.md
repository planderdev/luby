# Luby AI Advertiser Manual (Brand · Small Business)

> Audience: Advertisers who open campaigns to recruit creators · 최종 갱신 2026-08-19 · https://luby.im
> Agencies/operators should read this manual together with [03-Agency-Manual](03-대행사-매뉴얼.md).

## 1. Getting Started

### 1-1. Sign Up
1. At https://luby.im/signup, select **Advertiser** (or choose your role during onboarding after social login)
2. **Advertiser Type**: choose between `Brand/Small Business` / `Marketing Agency/Operator`
3. Enter company/business name, business registration number (10 digits), contact name and phone number → Sign up
4. Click the **email verification** link in your inbox → Enter the dashboard (advertisers can start immediately, no approval wait)

> Right after sign-up, you'll receive a **welcome notification** with a "Create your first campaign" link. On the FREE plan, your first campaign is free.

### 1-2. Fill Out Your Company Profile — `Dashboard → Settings`
- Contact name · Profile photo (logo) · **Advertiser Type** · Company name · Main industry · Company description · Website
- **Tax invoice / billing information** (representative name, business address, tax invoice recipient email) — used to issue tax invoices for BUSINESS plan payments. If not entered, invoices are issued to the contact email
- Follow the "Next: ○○" button on the **Company Profile Completeness** gauge at the top of Settings. Creators check the advertiser profile (`/dashboard/advertisers/[id]`) before applying to decide whether to trust you.

### 1-3. Reading the Dashboard Home
- Top cards: Registered campaigns · Recruiting · Current plan. Below, **Today's To-Dos** shows counts of pending selections, content awaiting review, unread messages, approaching deadlines, expiring subscriptions, and drafts.
- **Last 30-Day Funnel** (shown if you have at least one public campaign): Shows public page visits (unique visits · views) → applications → selections → published content with step-by-step conversion rates, plus traffic sources (QR poster, shared link, directory, referral, direct) and a link to your most-viewed campaign. For campaign-level detail, see Campaign Detail → Campaign Performance.

## 2. Creating a Campaign — `Dashboard → Campaigns → New Campaign`

### 2-1. Let AI Handle Everything (Recommended, ~20–40 sec)
1. In STEP 1, enter your **business name** and a **one-line industry description** (e.g., "K-beauty concept store in Myeongdong, 60% Chinese/Taiwanese tourists")
2. Click **Let AI Handle Everything** → A progress panel (showing elapsed time and steps) appears → Title, promotion type, category, channels, channel-specific missions, keywords, number of recruits, perks, and points are auto-filled
3. In STEP 3, the **recruitment period (start/end)** must be entered manually by the advertiser (AI does not set this arbitrarily). Trial period, day/time, same-day booking, and open recruitment options are also set here
4. Review and edit each step → **Request Review** in STEP 5 (or **Save Draft**)

> If you add **Xiaohongshu (小红书) · Douyin · Lemon8** as channels, AI writes missions in local formats (笔记 posts, hashtags, bilingual Chinese keywords). For stores targeting Chinese/overseas Chinese tourists, K-beauty, or K-food, Xiaohongshu is recommended.

### 2-2. Create Manually
- 5-step builder: **Basic Info → Promotion/Channels/Mission → Trial Schedule → Recruitment Settings/Keywords → Perks/Points**. Each step has an "AI Suggest" button to get suggestions for just that field.
- If required fields are empty, a modal will guide you to the relevant step when saving.
- **Benchmarks**: Below the recruitment headcount in Step 4, you'll see the median headcount, average competition ratio, fill rate, and commonly used channels from campaigns in the same category over the last 180 days (platform-wide if fewer than 3 samples). Below points in Step 5, you'll see the median points, typical range (25–75%), and approval rate. If your input falls outside the range, a short comment appears (e.g., "Below average — consider raising points if applications are low"). AI suggestions (full automation · headcount · points) are also based on the same benchmarks and suggest realistic ranges (categories with low competition get points suggested toward the higher end). Only aggregate figures are shown; individual campaigns of other advertisers are never exposed.

### 2-3. Review → Start Recruiting
- After requesting review, operators check it (usually same day). **Upon approval**, you'll receive a notification/email, and the campaign status becomes `Recruiting`
- **If rejected (revision requested)**: You'll get a notification/email with the operations team's requested changes, and the same message appears in a red banner at the top of the campaign detail page → **"Revise and Request Review Again"** → The request remains pinned at the top of the builder, and saving automatically submits a re-review request (Nth round)
- Status: `Draft → Under Review → Recruiting → Closed → Completed` / `Rejected (Revision Needed)` / `Cancelled`
- **Share Performance Report** (agency → client reporting): In the "Campaign Performance" section of campaign detail, click **Share Report** → Create share link → Send the `luby.im/r/…` link to your client/team. It opens without login and generates up-to-date figures each time (recruitment progress, competition ratio, estimated reach, published content, funnel, reach by channel, content links). Creator contact info and settlement details are not included, and there's a "Save as PDF" button for printing/PDF. Clicking "Turn off sharing" immediately invalidates the link. On the BUSINESS plan, **an AI summary is automatically generated once when a campaign is completed**, and clicking **AI Summary → Generate/Regenerate Summary** in the same panel produces a one-line result, interpretation, and next-step suggestion based on aggregated data, shown at the top of the report and in the performance section ("Regenerate" if figures change)
- **Adjusting an Active Campaign**: In the "Extend Recruitment · Add Headcount" panel on the campaign detail page, you can only **extend the recruitment deadline** and **increase recruitment headcount** (shortening, reducing headcount, or content edits are locked to protect applicants). To change content, cancel and duplicate the campaign to go through review again

### 2-4. Edit · Duplicate · Cancel
- **Edit** (Draft, Under Review, or Rejected status): Click **Edit** at the top of the detail page → The builder opens pre-filled with existing values → Save (as draft) or **Request Review**. For rejected campaigns, use the **"Revise and Request Again"** button to edit and resubmit the same campaign. Once recruitment has started, use duplication instead of editing
- **Create New from This Campaign** (top of detail page): Pre-fills channels, missions, keywords, perks, and schedule; only the recruitment period needs to be set again
- **Cancel Campaign** (Under Review, Recruiting, or Closed status): Sends cancellation notices to applicants and auto-cancels pending applications. Cancellation is not possible if points have already been paid to any applicant (status remains Closed). Campaigns with applicants cannot be deleted.

## 3. Boosting Recruitment

### 3-1. Shared Link (public page visible without login)

![Public campaign page shown via shared link (luby.im/c/…) — visible without login and leads to sign-up/application](/docs-img/campaign.jpg)
- From the top of the detail page, click **Shared Link ▾** → Select Korean (KR) / English (EN) / 中文(CN) → Copy and paste it into Instagram, KakaoTalk, Xiaohongshu, or your website
- The public page (`luby.im/c/…`) shows the mission, perks, points, schedule, and a **"Sign Up & Apply"** button, so new creators can apply right away.
- **English and Chinese pages are automatically translated** (`luby.im/en/c/…`, `luby.im/zh/c/…`). When a campaign is approved for recruiting or its content is edited, the title, business name, brief, mission, perks, and keywords are re-translated by AI so overseas creators see them in their language. If a translation feels awkward, clarify the original text and it will be re-translated automatically. A **Translated** badge next to the EN/CN items in the Shared Link menu means it's ready, and the store QR poster also prints with translated title/perks when EN/CN is selected.
- **In-Store QR Poster**: In the same popover, click **Print A4 In-Store QR Poster** → A vertical A4 poster opens with the campaign title, business name, perks, points, recruitment headcount, deadline, channels, and a QR code to the public page. Add `?lang=en`/`zh` for English/Chinese versions. Use the **Print / Save as PDF** button to print and post at the counter, entrance, or tables so walk-in customers can scan the QR and apply (only the campaign owner/operator can open this).
- **Traffic Source Tracking**: Public page views from shared links, QR posters, the campaign directory, and creator referral links are tracked automatically. At the top of **Campaign Performance → Progress Funnel** on the campaign detail page, "Public Page Visits" (unique visitors) and the visit-to-application conversion rate are shown, with view counts by traffic source below — this also feeds into shared reports and AI summaries. No personal data is stored (visitors are distinguished only by a daily-rotating hash), and views from you viewing your own campaign are excluded. When your public page gets English or Chinese views, view counts **by language** are shown as well, so you can see how many Chinese creators a Xiaohongshu campaign is reaching.

### 3-2. AI Creator Matching · Invitations (BUSINESS)
- At the bottom of the detail page, click **AI Matching** → Candidates appear with fit scores and reasons for recommendation → Click **Invite**. If the creator accepts, they are automatically entered as an applicant.

### 3-3. Creator Search — `Dashboard → Find Creators`
- Search terms (name · bio) · Industry · Region · Channel · Followers · Sort (followers/completed count/most recent)
- **Quick Filters**: `Xiaohongshu` `Douyin` `Lemon8` `Has Public Profile`
- Click a card → View portfolio (channels · completion history) → **Invite** (select a recruiting campaign)

## 4. Selecting Applicants → Trial → Content Review — Campaign Detail

1. **Applicant List**: Check channel, followers, and application message → `Select` / `Not Selected`. On the FREE plan, you can view up to 10 applicants
2. Selected creators are notified. Use **Chat** (BUSINESS) to coordinate schedule/instructions (`Dashboard → Messages`)
3. When a creator submits a content URL, you're notified. Use **AI Pre-Review** on the applicant's row to first check channel match and mission checkpoints
4. **Approve** → Points are paid instantly to the creator (atomic) · **Request Revision** → Send feedback → Resubmission → Re-review
5. Once all selected creators are approved (after the deadline), the campaign is **automatically completed** and a **performance report notification** arrives (applications, selections, approvals, points paid, estimated reach)

> If review is delayed more than 3 days, you'll get a reminder; if selection is delayed more than 3 days after the deadline, you'll get a "N applicants are waiting to be selected" notification.

## 5. Viewing Performance
- **Campaign Performance** card on campaign detail: Recruitment progress, application competition ratio, estimated reach (sum of selected creators' followers), points paid, funnel, reach by channel
- Campaign list card chips: `Pending Selection N` `Applications N` `Selected N/Target` `Approved N · Paid P`

## 6. Subscription · Billing — `Dashboard → Subscription & Billing`
- FREE: 1 free campaign · view up to 10 applicants / BUSINESS: Unlimited campaigns · AI matching/invite/chat · unlimited applicant viewing / ENTERPRISE: Contact sales
- Payment via Toss Payments checkout (card). 30-day subscription, notification 7 days before expiry, auto-downgrade to FREE upon expiry
- Check individual transactions at the bottom of the Subscription & Billing page under **Payment History**. Card receipts serve as valid proof of payment; if you need a tax invoice, click **"Request Tax Invoice"** → The operations team issues it using the tax invoice information in Settings and notifies you

## 7. Notification/Email Settings — `Settings → Email Notifications`
- In-app notifications are always received. Email notifications can be toggled on/off separately for **Progress Updates** and **Reminders/Recommendations**. Every notification email includes an unsubscribe link at the bottom.

## 8. Frequently Asked Questions
- **My campaign is stuck in Under Review** → It's awaiting operator review. If you haven't received a rejection notice, it will be processed soon.
- **I can't see applicants (limit of 10)** → This is a FREE plan limitation. Upgrade to BUSINESS.
- **I don't like the AI draft** → Click again to get a new draft. The more specific your industry description (location, customers, key products, target country), the better the result.
- **Applications keep coming in after the recruitment period has ended** → If `Open Recruitment` is enabled, the campaign won't auto-close. Turn it off in STEP 3, or cancel/close the campaign.

### Weekly Performance Digest
Every **Monday at 9 AM**, you'll receive a single notification/email summarizing last week's campaign performance (new applications, selections, content submissions, approvals, this week's deadlines, pending reviews, completed campaigns). You can turn this off under **Digest** in Settings > Email Notification Settings.

### AI Applicant Fit Scoring (BUSINESS)
Clicking the **AI Fit Scoring** button on the right of the applicant list on the campaign detail page evaluates pending applicants (up to 30 per run) against campaign criteria (required channel, category, region, followers relative to reward, application message, trial history), displaying a score from 0–100, reasoning, and points to verify before selection (e.g., international shipping availability), sorted by score. The button reappears when new applications come in, and you can re-run evaluation on all applicants with "Re-evaluate." After evaluation, the **Select Top N** button lets you batch-select applicants by score (default: remaining recruitment headcount), though the final decision remains with the advertiser.

### AI Usage Limits
AI features (campaign creation, matching, applicant fit scoring, content review, application drafts, report summaries) have a **monthly limit** per account — 30 for FREE, 300 for BUSINESS, unlimited for ENTERPRISE. Limits reset on the 1st of each month, and you can check your usage for the current month on the Billing/Plan page. Once the limit is reached, a notice appears and AI features are locked until next month (manual features remain available).

### Push Notifications
Clicking **Settings > Push Notifications > Enable on This Device** lets you receive application, selection, and review notifications directly on your device even when the app is closed (categories from your email settings apply equally to push notifications). This works immediately on Android and PC Chrome; **on iPhone, enable it from the Luby AI app installed via Safari Share → Add to Home Screen**. Can be turned on/off per device.

### Campaign Duplication + Applying Past Performance (AI)
When you create a new campaign via Duplicate from campaign detail, a **"Re-suggest Using Past Performance"** button appears in Step 1. It creates a draft with adjusted headcount, points, channels, and missions based on the original campaign's competition ratio, approval rate, and reach by channel, and shows 2–4 lines explaining what changed and why (e.g., "Competition ratio was low at 0.4:1, so points were raised from 15,000 to 20,000P"). This counts toward your monthly AI limit. If **7 days** have passed since a campaign was completed and no new campaign has been created, you'll receive a one-time notification asking "Would you like us to suggest your next campaign based on past performance?" — clicking the link takes you directly to this screen (can be turned off under the Reminders/Recommendations category).

## 9. Xiaohongshu Seeding Campaign Guide — Chinese Influencer Seeding

Xiaohongshu (小红书) is the go-to channel for Chinese and overseas Chinese customers researching travel, dining, and beauty information about Korea. Luby AI is built to carry the experience through in Chinese — from campaign registration to the Chinese-language public page, and Chinese creators' sign-up, application, and notifications — so Korean stores and brands can run Xiaohongshu seeding campaigns without a separate Chinese agency.

### 9-1. Creating a Campaign — Add Xiaohongshu as a Channel
- In your STEP 1 industry description, **clearly state your target audience** (e.g., "K-beauty concept store in Myeongdong, 60% Chinese/Taiwanese tourists," "Jeju black pork restaurant aiming for Xiaohongshu exposure"). Clicking **Let AI Handle Everything** will include Xiaohongshu as a channel and write missions in 笔记 format (photo count, hashtags, bilingual Chinese keywords).
- Be sure to include in the mission: ① At least one 笔记 post (6–9 photos or 1 video) ② Store location tag (定位) and the Chinese name of your business ③ 2–3 designated search-intent hashtags (e.g., #韩国旅行 #济州美食) ④ Deadline for submitting the link after posting
- In Keywords (STEP 4), include both Korean and Chinese. These are used as-is on the public page, Chinese translation, and for search visibility.
- Perks/Points (STEP 5): Since point withdrawal is **based on Korean bank accounts**, **perks** like free trials, products, or gifts are a bigger incentive for overseas creators. You can run a campaign with points set to 0, relying solely on perks.

### 9-2. Promoting in Chinese — Shared Link, QR Poster, Preview Cards
- Once approved, the public page is **automatically translated into Chinese** (`luby.im/zh/c/…`). Copy **Shared Link ▾ → 中文(CN)** from the top of the detail page and post it to Xiaohongshu 笔记, WeChat, or local communities. The preview image (OG card) shown when sharing also appears in Chinese.
- Print the **QR Poster 中文(CN)** for your store. When visiting Chinese tourists scan it, the Chinese campaign page opens, and sign-up and application proceed entirely in Chinese.
- Public page views are tracked by language and traffic source (shared link, QR, directory, referral), visible at the top of the campaign performance funnel.

### 9-3. Finding and Inviting Chinese Creators
- Turn on the **Xiaohongshu** quick filter in `Find Creators` to see only creators who have registered a Xiaohongshu channel. Check their channel and completion history in their portfolio, then **Invite** them (BUSINESS plan's AI Matching recommends by fit score).
- When creators select Xiaohongshu under Settings → Channels, they're guided to enter their 小红书号 and profile share link. Approval proceeds once the operations team reviews the channel.

### 9-4. Trial → 笔记 Publication → Review
- After selection, coordinate the visit schedule and publishing requirements via **Messages**. Chinese creators view the interface in Chinese, but **message content is not translated**, so it helps to include simple Chinese or English in your messages.
- Once the creator submits the 笔记 link, it goes to review → Upon approval, perks and points are finalized. Since Xiaohongshu 笔记 posts are difficult to edit after publishing, we recommend **requesting revisions at the draft stage before publishing**, via messages.
- If you've independently recruited Xiaohongshu creators outside of Luby, let the operations team know (contact@plander.io). We'll include the results in your campaign performance as external results.

### 9-5. Checklist
- [ ] Industry description specifies target country/platform
- [ ] Xiaohongshu is added as a channel, and the mission includes 笔记 format, location tag, and hashtags
- [ ] Keywords include Chinese
- [ ] Perks are appealing to overseas creators (not relying on points alone)
- [ ] After approval, the Chinese shared link and QR poster (CN) have been distributed
