# Luby AI Advertiser Manual (Brands & Small Businesses)

> Target audience: Advertisers who open campaigns to recruit creators · 최종 갱신 2026-08-19 · https://luby.im
> Agencies should refer to this manual along with the [03-Agency-Manual](03-대행사-매뉴얼.md).

## 1. Getting Started

### 1-1. Sign Up
1. At https://luby.im/signup, select **Advertiser** (or choose your role during onboarding after social login)
2. **Advertiser type**: choose `Brand · Small Business` or `Marketing Agency · Operator`
3. Enter your company/business name, business registration number (10 digits), and the contact person's name and phone number → Sign up
4. Click the **email verification** link in your inbox → You'll be taken to the dashboard (advertisers can start using the service immediately, with no approval wait)

> Right after signing up, you'll receive a **welcome notification** with a "Create your first campaign" link. On the FREE plan, your first campaign is free.

### 1-2. Complete Your Company Profile — `Dashboard → Settings`
- Contact name · Profile picture (logo) · **Advertiser type** · Company name · Main industry · Company description · Website
- **Tax invoice / billing information** (representative name, business address, tax invoice email) — used to issue tax invoices for BUSINESS plan payments. If left blank, invoices are sent to the contact's email
- Follow the "Next: ○○" button on the **company profile completeness** gauge at the top of Settings. Creators check the advertiser profile (`/dashboard/advertisers/[id]`) before applying to decide whether to trust the campaign.

### 1-3. Reading the Dashboard Home
- Top cards: registered campaigns, currently recruiting, current plan. Below, **Today's To-Dos** shows counts of applicants awaiting selection, content awaiting review, unread messages, upcoming deadlines, subscription expiration, and drafts.
- **30-Day Funnel** (shown if you have at least one public campaign): displays public page visits (unique visits/views) → applications → selections → published content, along with the conversion rate at each stage, traffic sources (QR poster, share link, directory, referral, direct), and a link to your most-viewed campaign. For details per campaign, check Campaign Details → Campaign Performance.

## 2. Creating a Campaign — `Dashboard → Campaigns → New Campaign`

### 2-1. Let AI Handle Everything (Recommended, ~20–40 seconds)
1. In STEP 1, enter your **business name** and a **one-line description of your business** (e.g., "K-beauty concept store in Myeongdong, 60% Chinese/Taiwanese tourists")
2. Click **Let AI Handle Everything** → a progress panel (elapsed time and stage) appears → title, promotion type, category, channels, channel-specific missions, keywords, number of recruits, offerings, and points are auto-filled
3. In STEP 3, the **recruitment period (start/end dates)** must be entered by the advertiser (AI will not set this arbitrarily). Trial period, days/hours, same-day booking, and always-open recruitment options are also set here
4. Review and adjust each step → click **Request Review** in STEP 5 (or **Save as Draft**)

> If you include **Xiaohongshu (小红书), Douyin, or Lemon8** as channels, the AI will write missions in the local format (笔记 posts, hashtags, bilingual Chinese keywords). Xiaohongshu is recommended for stores or K-beauty/K-food businesses targeting Chinese-speaking tourists.

### 2-2. Manual Creation
- 5-step builder: **Basic Info → Promotion/Channels/Mission → Experience Schedule → Recruitment Settings & Keywords → Offerings & Points**. Each step has an "AI Suggest" button to get suggestions for just that field.
- If required fields are missing, a modal will guide you to the relevant step when saving.
- **Benchmarks**: Below the recruitment count in Step 4, you'll see the median recruitment count, average competition ratio, fill rate, and commonly used channels for campaigns in the same category over the past 180 days (or platform-wide if fewer than 3 samples exist). Below points in Step 5, you'll see the median point value, typical range (25th–75th percentile), and approval rate. If your input falls outside the typical range, a short comment appears (e.g., "Below average — consider raising points if applications are low"). AI suggestions (Let AI Handle Everything, recruitment count, points) are also based on the same benchmarks and propose realistic values within range (for categories with low competition, points are suggested toward the upper end). Only aggregated figures are shown — individual campaigns from other advertisers are never exposed.

### 2-3. Review → Start Recruiting
- After requesting review, an operator will check it (usually same day). If **approved**, you'll receive a **notification/email**, and the campaign status changes to `Recruiting`
- If **rejected (revision requested)**: you'll receive a notification/email with the operations team's requested changes, and the same message appears in a red banner at the top of the campaign detail page → click **"Revise and Request Review Again"** → the requested changes stay pinned at the top of the builder, and saving automatically submits a re-review request (attempt N)
- Status flow: `Draft → Under Review → Recruiting → Closed → Completed` / `Rejected (Revision Needed)` / `Cancelled`
- **Sharing Performance Reports** (for agencies reporting to clients): In the "Campaign Performance" section of Campaign Details, click **Share Report** → create a share link → send the `luby.im/r/…` link to your client or team. It opens without login, and generates the latest figures each time it's opened (recruitment progress, competition ratio, estimated reach, published content, funnel, reach by channel, content links). Creator contact and payment info are not included, and there's a "Save as PDF" button for printing/PDF export. Clicking "Turn off sharing" invalidates the link immediately. On the BUSINESS plan, an **AI summary is automatically generated once when a campaign is completed**; clicking **AI Summary → Generate/Regenerate Summary** in the same panel produces a one-line result, interpretation, and next-step suggestion based on the aggregated data, shown at the top of the report and in the performance section (click "Regenerate" if the figures change)
- **Adjusting an Active Campaign**: In the "Extend Recruitment · Add Recruits" panel on the campaign detail page, you can only **extend the recruitment deadline** and **increase the number of recruits** (shortening, reducing, or editing content is locked to protect applicants). To change the content, cancel the campaign and duplicate it to request a new review

### 2-4. Edit · Duplicate · Cancel
- **Edit** (for Draft, Under Review, or Rejected status): Click the **Edit** button at the top of the detail page → the builder opens pre-filled with existing values → save as Draft or click **Request Review**. For rejected campaigns, use the **"Revise and Request Again"** button to fix and resubmit the same campaign. Once recruitment has started, use duplication instead of editing
- **Create New from This Campaign** (top of detail page): pre-fills channels, missions, keywords, offerings, and schedule, letting you only set a new recruitment period
- **Cancel Campaign** (available for Under Review, Recruiting, or Closed status): sends a cancellation notice to applicants and automatically cancels pending applications. Cannot be cancelled if any applicant has already been paid points (status remains Closed). Campaigns with applicants cannot be deleted.

## 3. Boosting Recruitment

### 3-1. Share Links (Public Page Visible Without Login)

![A public campaign page opened via a share link (luby.im/c/…) — visible without login and leads to sign-up and application](/docs-img/campaign.jpg)
- At the top of the detail page, click **Share Link ▾** → choose Korean (KR) / English (EN) / Chinese (CN) and copy → paste into Instagram, KakaoTalk, Xiaohongshu, or your website
- The public page (`luby.im/c/…`) shows the mission, offerings, points, schedule, and a **"Sign Up & Apply"** button, so new creators can go straight from discovery to application.
- **In-Store QR Poster**: In the same popover, click **Print A4 In-Store QR Poster** → a vertical A4 poster opens showing the campaign title, business name, offer, points, number of recruits, deadline, channel, and a QR code linking to the public page. English/Chinese versions are available via `?lang=en`/`zh`. Use the **Print / Save as PDF** button to print it directly and post it at the counter, entrance, or tables so visiting customers can scan the QR code and apply (only accessible to the campaign owner/operator).
- **Traffic Source Tracking**: Public page views coming from share links, QR posters, the campaign directory, and creator referral links are automatically tracked. At the top of **Campaign Performance → Progress Funnel** in Campaign Details, you'll see "Public Page Visits" (unique visitors) and the visit-to-application conversion rate, followed by view counts by traffic source — this data also feeds into shared reports and AI summaries. No personal data is stored (visitors are distinguished only by a daily-rotating hash), and views of your own campaign by you are excluded.

### 3-2. AI Creator Matching & Invitations (BUSINESS)
- At the bottom of the detail page, click **AI Matching** → view candidates with fit scores and recommendation reasons → click **Invite**. If the creator accepts, they're automatically added as an applicant.

### 3-3. Creator Search — `Dashboard → Find Creators`
- Search by keyword (name/bio) · industry · region · channel · follower count · sort (followers/completions/latest)
- **Quick Filters**: `Xiaohongshu` `Douyin` `Lemon8` `Has Public Profile`
- Click a card → view portfolio (channels, completion history) → **Invite** (select an active campaign)

## 4. Selecting Applicants → Experience → Content Review — Campaign Details

1. **Applicant List**: check channel, followers, and application message → mark as `Selected` / `Not Selected`. FREE plan allows viewing up to 10 applicants
2. Selected creators receive a notification. Use **Chat** (BUSINESS) to coordinate schedule and instructions (`Dashboard → Messages`)
3. When a creator submits a content URL, you'll be notified. Use **AI Pre-Review** in the applicant's row to check channel match and mission checkpoints first
4. **Approve** → points are paid to the creator immediately (atomic transaction) · **Request Revision** → send feedback → resubmission → re-review
5. Once all selected applicants are approved (after the deadline), the campaign is **automatically completed** and you'll receive a **performance report notification** (applications, selections, approvals, points paid, estimated reach)

> If review is pending for 3+ days, you'll get a reminder; if selection is pending for 3+ days after the deadline, you'll be notified "N applicants are waiting to be selected."

## 5. Viewing Performance
- **Campaign Performance** card in Campaign Details: recruitment progress, application competition ratio, estimated reach (sum of selected creators' followers), points paid, funnel, reach by channel
- Chips on campaign list cards: `Awaiting Selection N` `Applications N` `Selected N/Target` `Approved N · Paid P`

## 6. Subscription & Billing — `Dashboard → Subscription & Billing`
- FREE: 1 free campaign · view up to 10 applicants / BUSINESS: unlimited campaigns · AI matching/invitations/chat · unlimited applicants / ENTERPRISE: contact sales
- Payments are made via Toss Payments checkout (card). 30-day subscription, notification 7 days before expiration, auto-downgrade to FREE upon expiration
- Check payment history per transaction at the bottom of the Subscription & Billing page under **Payment History**. Card payments come with a card sales receipt as valid documentation; if you need a tax invoice, click **"Request Tax Invoice"** → the operations team will issue it based on the tax invoice information in Settings and notify you

## 7. Notification & Email Settings — `Settings → Email Notifications`
- In-app notifications are always received. Emails can be toggled on/off separately for two categories: **Progress Notifications / Reminders & Recommendations**. All notification emails include an unsubscribe link at the bottom.

## 8. Frequently Asked Questions
- **My campaign is stuck in review** → It's awaiting operator review. If you haven't received a rejection notice, it should be processed soon.
- **I can't see all applicants (limited to 10)** → This is a FREE plan limit. Upgrade to BUSINESS to remove it.
- **I don't like the AI draft** → Click again to generate a new draft. The more specific your business description (location, customers, key products, target country), the better the results.
- **Applications keep coming in even though the recruitment period has passed** → If `Always Open` is enabled, the campaign won't close automatically. Turn it off in STEP 3, or cancel/close the campaign manually.

### Weekly Performance Digest
Every **Monday at 9 AM**, you'll receive a single notification/email summarizing last week's campaign performance (new applications, selections, content submissions, approvals, this week's deadlines, pending reviews, completed campaigns). You can turn this off under **Digest** in Settings > Email Notification Settings.

### AI Applicant Fit Score (BUSINESS)
Clicking the **AI Fit Score** button next to the applicant list in Campaign Details evaluates pending applicants (up to 30 per run) against campaign criteria (required channels, category, region, followers relative to compensation, application message, experience history), giving each a score from 0–100 with rationale and points to verify before selection (e.g., whether international shipping is possible), sorted by score. The button reappears when new applications arrive, and you can click "Re-evaluate" to reassess all applicants. After evaluation, you can also use the **Select Top N** button to bulk-select applicants by score (defaults to the number of remaining recruitment slots) — final decisions remain with the advertiser.

### AI Usage Limits
AI features (campaign creation, matching, applicant fit scoring, content review, application drafts, report summaries) have a **monthly limit** per account — 30 for FREE, 300 for BUSINESS, and unlimited for ENTERPRISE. Limits reset on the 1st of each month, and you can check this month's usage on the Billing/Plan page. When the limit is reached, a notice appears and AI features are locked until the next month (manual features remain available).

### Push Notifications
Clicking **Push Notifications > Turn on for this device** in Settings lets you receive application, selection, and review notifications directly on your device even when the app is closed (the categories set in Email Notification Settings apply to push as well). This works immediately on Android and PC Chrome; on **iPhone, it can be enabled from the Luby AI app installed via Safari's Share → Add to Home Screen**. Can be turned on/off per device.

### Campaign Duplication + Applying Past Performance (AI)
When you create a new campaign via Duplicate in Campaign Details, a **"Re-suggest Based on Past Performance"** button appears in Step 1. It creates a draft with adjusted recruitment count, points, channels, and mission based on the original campaign's application competition ratio, approval rate, and reach by channel, and shows a 2–4 line summary of what was changed and why (e.g., "Competition ratio was low at 0.4:1, so points were raised from 15,000 to 20,000P"). This counts toward your monthly AI usage limit. If **7 days have passed since a campaign was completed** and you haven't created a new one, you'll receive a one-time notification asking "Would you like a suggestion for your next campaign based on past performance?" — clicking the link takes you directly to this screen (can be turned off under the Reminders & Recommendations category).
