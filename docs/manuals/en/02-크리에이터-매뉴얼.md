# Luby AI Creator Manual

> Audience: Creators who apply to campaigns, complete experiences, publish content, and earn points · Last updated 최종 갱신 2026-08-19 · https://luby.im

> Pre-signup info page: **luby.im/creators** (EN: /en/creators, CN: /zh/creators) — See the number of campaigns currently open, remaining spots, point levels, and how it works without logging in. Use this link when sharing with friends.

## 1. Sign Up and Approval

> **Language**: The signup, login, and password screens support Korean, English, and Chinese. Switching **KR / EN / CN** at the top right remembers your choice, and if you arrive from an English or Chinese campaign page, it opens directly in that language. After logging in, Home (overview, today's tasks, recommended campaigns), the menu, campaign browsing (list/filters), campaign details, applications, and My Applications (content submission) all display in the same language, and English/Chinese users also see campaign titles and content translated. The entire creator experience — Settings (channels, specialties, public profile, notifications), received invitations, points/withdrawals, messages, and portfolio — appears in the same language. Notification emails are still sent in Korean for now.
1. Go to https://luby.im/signup and select **Creator** (or continue with Google/Kakao → select Creator during onboarding)
2. Enter your name/nickname, region, main channel (URL), and specialties (up to 3) → Sign Up → **verify your email**
3. An operator will review your channel/profile and **approve** it (usually within 24 hours). Once you receive an approval notification/email, you can apply to campaigns. You can browse campaigns even before approval.

## 2. Complete Your Profile — `Dashboard → Settings`
Fill the **Profile Completeness** gauge at the top of the Overview Home and Settings to 100%. A more complete profile means more recommendations and invitations.

| Item | Location | Tip |
|---|---|---|
| SNS Channel (30%) | Settings → Channels (`#channels`) | Selecting a channel type changes the URL/handle example. For **Xiaohongshu**, the handle is the `小红书号` from your app profile, and the URL is your profile share link (xhslink short links allowed) · Douyin uses `抖音号` · Lemon8 uses `lemon8-app.com/@…`. Addresses that don't match the selected channel type won't be saved |
| Specialties (25%) | Settings → Specialties (`#categories`) | Up to 3. Campaigns matching your specialties are recommended first |
| Follower Count (15%) | Entered per channel | Used by advertiser filters and estimated reach calculations |
| One-line Intro (15%) | Settings | The first sentence advertisers read when selecting creators (10+ characters) |
| Region (10%) · Profile Photo (5%) | Settings | Used for region-based recommendations · Profiles with photos have higher selection rates |

## 3. Find Campaigns — `Dashboard → Campaigns`

![Campaign directory — channel/sort filters and spots-remaining badge](/docs-img/directory.jpg)
- Default sort is **Recommended** (reflects your specialties, region, and application status), badges for `N spots left` (campaigns with fewer applicants than recruitment slots, so higher selection odds), `My Specialty`, `My Region`, `Applied` / sort by newest, deadline soon, or points, plus industry/region filters
- The public directory (luby.im/c, including EN/CN), viewable without logging in, also supports sorting by **deadline soon, newest, points** and channel/region filters
- Overview Home shows **"Campaigns For You"** (3 picks), the number of new campaigns this week, and a morning alert if **a campaign in your specialty closes within 48 hours**
- **Instant new campaign alerts**: When a campaign matching your specialty (or your region + your channels) opens for recruitment, you'll get an immediate notification/push/email (up to 1 per day, the rest bundled into the Monday digest). The more complete your specialties, region, and channels, the more accurate this gets. You can turn it off under **Reminders & Recommendations** in `Settings → Email Notifications`.
- Campaign details: mission (per channel), what's provided, recruitment period, points, and advertiser profile link
- The **Selection Likelihood** card on the right of campaign details shows a High/Medium/Low rating with improvement tips (register a channel, add a specialty, write an application message) based on competition ratio (applicants/recruitment, D-day), your match with specialty/region/channel, and completion history. This is a reference estimate only — final selection is made by the advertiser

## 4. Apply · Invitations

![Public campaign page — points, recruitment deadline, sign up and apply](/docs-img/campaign.jpg)
1. On the details page, select **Apply** → write an application message (optional). Pressing the **AI Draft** button fills in 2–3 sentences based on the campaign mission and your profile (editable). If competition is high, differentiate with a specific plan, write around channels you actually have, and never invent channels you haven't registered or experience you don't have. Completed experiences on Luby AI can be included as credibility evidence
2. While your application is pending, you can **Cancel Application**. You cannot apply more than once to the same campaign
3. If an advertiser invites you, go to `Dashboard → Received Invitations` to **Accept (auto-applies)** / **Decline**
4. If selected, you'll get a notification/email → coordinate schedule with the advertiser via **Chat** (`Dashboard → Messages`)

## 5. Experience → Content Submission → Review — `Dashboard → My Applications`
1. Publish content following the mission of the selected campaign (**must disclose sponsorship, e.g. #ad #sponsored**)
2. **Submit Content**: publication URL + notes → advertiser review
3. **Approved** → points credited instantly / **Revision Requested** → check feedback → **resubmit** on the same screen
4. If there's no submission after the experience period ends, you'll receive a reminder

## 6. Points and Withdrawals — `Dashboard → Points`
- **Point Balance** (1P = 1 KRW) · **Point History**: content approvals (+campaign name), referral rewards, withdrawals, pending withdrawals, rejections, shown chronologically
- **Request Withdrawal**: minimum 10,000P, enter bank/account/account holder name → deducted immediately upon request → paid after operator confirmation (if rejected, automatically refunded with a reason shown)

## 7. Public Profile · Friend Referral
- Toggle `Settings → Public Profile` ON → get a `luby.im/p/your-ID` link (KR/EN/CN) — shows only name, photo, intro, region, specialties, channels, and completed collaborations (email/contact/payout info stays private). Send this as your portfolio to advertisers/overseas brands
- In `Dashboard → My Portfolio`, you can save it as a PDF and **print a QR business card on A4** → 10 business-card-sized (90×55mm) prints fit on one sheet; scanning the QR opens your public profile (requires Public Profile ON). Hand it directly to shop owners during store visits, events, or pop-ups
- When Public Profile is ON, the top of **My Portfolio** shows profile view counts (total, unique visitors, last 7 days) and traffic sources (QR card / portfolio link / direct/search). Visitors are identified only by a daily-rotating hash with no personal data stored, and your own views are excluded
- From the **Invite Friends, Get 500P** card on Home, copy/share your referral link (luby.im/creators?ref=…) and check signups, rewards, and this month's count. On campaign details, use **Share with Friends** → copy a campaign-specific referral link (?ref). When a friend who signs up via your link **completes their first experience, you get 500P** (up to 5 per month). Check this under "My Referral Signups" on Overview Home

## 8. Notifications · Using It Like an App
- In-app notifications + email (progress alerts / reminders & recommendations, each toggleable under `Settings → Email Notifications`)
- On a mobile browser, go to luby.im → **Add to Home Screen** → runs like an app (shortcuts: Campaigns, My Applications, Notifications)

## 9. FAQ
- **I'm not getting approved** → Check whether your channel URL is a real profile and whether your intro/specialties are filled in. If waiting more than 24 hours, contact contact@plander.io
- **I don't see an Apply button** → You're either not yet approved, or the campaign's recruitment has closed
- **I haven't received my points** → Points are credited after advertiser approval. Check the status (Submitted/Revision Requested/Approved) in My Applications

### Weekly Digest
Every **Monday at 9 AM**, you'll receive a single notification/email with newly opened campaigns in your specialty/region, deadlines this week, counts of pending applications/submissions needed/under review, last week's approved and earned points, (if Public Profile is ON) last week's profile views and QR card traffic, and withdrawal eligibility. You can turn this off under **Digest** in Settings > Email Notification Settings.

### Push Notifications
Tap Settings > **Push Notifications > Turn on for this device** to receive selection, approval, and deadline alerts directly on your device even while the app is closed (the categories in your email notification settings also apply to push). This works immediately on Android and PC Chrome; on **iPhone, you can turn it on from the Luby AI app installed via Safari's Share → Add to Home Screen**. It can be turned on/off per device.

### My Portfolio (PDF)
From Menu > **My Portfolio**, view your profile, channels, specialties, and collaboration history in a portfolio format to show brands, and **save as PDF or print**. You can view it yourself even with Public Profile off; turning it on lets you share the same view via a link (`luby.im/p/…`).
