# Luby AI Creator Manual

> Audience: Creators who apply to campaigns, complete experiences, publish content, and earn points · Last updated 최종 갱신 2026-08-19 · https://luby.im

> Pre-signup info page: **luby.im/creators** (EN: /en/creators, CN: /zh/creators) — View the number of campaigns currently recruiting, remaining spots, point levels, and how it works, without logging in. Use this link when sharing with friends.

## 1. Sign Up and Approval

> **Language**: The signup, login, and password screens support Korean, English, and Chinese. Switching to **KR / EN / CN** at the top right remembers your choice, and if you arrive from an English or Chinese campaign page, it opens directly in that language. After logging in, the Home (overview, today's tasks, recommended campaigns), Menu, Campaign Browse (list/filters), Campaign Details, Apply, and My Applications (content submission) screens all display in the same language, and English/Chinese users also see translated campaign titles and content. Other screens (Settings, Messages, Points, etc.) are still Korean-only.
1. Select **Creator** at https://luby.im/signup (or continue with Google/Kakao → select Creator during onboarding)
2. Enter your name/nickname, activity region, main channel (URL), specialties (up to 3) → Sign up → **verify your email**
3. An admin will review your channel and profile, then **approve** it (usually within 24 hours). Once you receive an approval notification/email, you can apply. You can browse campaigns even before approval.

## 2. Completing Your Profile — `Dashboard → Settings`
Bring the **Profile Completeness** gauge at the top of the overview home and settings to 100%. A higher completeness score leads to more recommendations and invitations.

| Item | Location | Tip |
|---|---|---|
| SNS Channels (30%) | Settings → Channels (`#channels`) | Selecting a channel type changes the URL/handle example. For **Xiaohongshu**, the handle is the `小红书号` from your app profile, and the URL is your profile share link (xhslink short links allowed) · Douyin uses `抖音号` · Lemon8 uses `lemon8-app.com/@…`. Addresses that don't match the channel type won't be saved |
| Specialties (25%) | Settings → Specialties (`#categories`) | Up to 3. Campaigns in your specialties are recommended first |
| Follower Count (15%) | Entered per channel | Used by advertiser filters and estimated reach calculations |
| One-line Bio (15%) | Settings | The first sentence advertisers read when selecting creators (10+ characters) |
| Activity Region (10%) · Profile Photo (5%) | Settings | Used for region-based recommendations · Profiles with photos have higher selection rates |

## 3. Finding Campaigns — `Dashboard → Campaigns`

![Campaign directory — channel/sort filters and spots-remaining badges](/docs-img/directory.jpg)
- Default sort is **Recommended** (reflects your specialties, region, and application status). Badges include `N spots left` (campaigns with fewer applicants than recruiting slots, meaning higher selection odds), `My Specialty`, `My Region`, `Applied` / Sort by newest, closing soon, or points, with industry and region filters
- The public directory viewable without login (luby.im/c, including EN/CN) also supports **closing soon, newest, and points** sorting along with channel/region filters
- Overview home shows 3 **"Campaigns for You"** · number of new campaigns this week · a morning notification when **a campaign in your specialty is closing within 48 hours**
- **Instant new campaign alerts**: When a campaign matching your specialties (or your activity region + channels you own) starts recruiting, you'll get an immediate notification, push, and email (up to 1 per day, with the rest bundled into the Monday digest). The more complete your specialties, region, and channels, the more accurate this is. You can turn this off under **Reminders & Recommendations** in `Settings → Email Notifications`.
- Campaign Details: missions (by channel), what's provided, recruitment period, points, and advertiser profile link
- The **Selection Likelihood** card on the right of campaign details shows competition rate (applicants/recruiting slots, D-day), whether your specialty/region/channel match, and your completion history, along with a High/Medium/Low rating and improvement tips (register a channel, add a specialty, write an application message). This is an estimate for reference only; the advertiser makes the final selection

## 4. Applying · Invitations

![Public campaign page — points, recruitment deadline, and sign up & apply](/docs-img/campaign.jpg)
1. On the details page, click **Apply** → write an application message (optional). Clicking **AI Draft** fills in 2–3 sentences based on the campaign mission and your profile (editable). If competition is high, differentiate with a concrete plan, write mainly around the channels you own, and never fabricate channels you haven't registered or experience you don't have. The number of completed experiences on Luby AI can be used as proof of credibility
2. You can **cancel your application** while it's pending. Duplicate applications to the same campaign are not allowed
3. When invited by an advertiser, go to `Dashboard → Received Invitations` to **Accept (auto-apply)** / Decline
4. Once selected, you'll get a notification/email → coordinate schedules with the advertiser via **Chat** (`Dashboard → Messages`)

## 5. Experience → Content Submission → Review — `Dashboard → My Applications`
1. Publish content according to the mission of the selected campaign (**must include disclosure such as #Ad #Sponsored**)
2. **Submit Content**: Enter the published URL + notes → sent for advertiser review
3. **Approved** → points credited immediately / **Revision Requested** → check feedback → **resubmit** on the same screen
4. If no submission is made after the experience period ends, you'll receive a reminder

## 6. Points and Withdrawals — `Dashboard → Points`
- **Point Balance** (1P = 1 KRW) · **Point History**: content approvals (+ campaign name), referral rewards, withdrawals, pending withdrawals, and rejections, shown in chronological order
- **Withdrawal Request**: Minimum 10,000P. Enter bank, account number, and account holder name → points are deducted immediately upon request → paid out after admin confirmation (if rejected, automatically refunded with a reason shown)

## 7. Public Profile · Friend Referrals
- Toggle `Settings → Public Profile` ON → get a `luby.im/p/yourID` link (KR/EN/CN) showing only your name, photo, bio, region, specialties, channels, and completed collaborations (email and contact info stay private; payout info stays private). Send this as a portfolio to advertisers and overseas brands
- Under `Dashboard → My Portfolio`, save as PDF or **print a QR business card on A4** → 10 standard-size (90×55mm) business cards print on one sheet; scanning the QR opens your public profile (requires Public Profile to be ON). Hand these directly to shop owners during store visits, events, or pop-ups
- When your public profile is ON, the top of **My Portfolio** shows profile view counts (total, unique visitors, last 7 days) and traffic sources (QR card / portfolio link / direct/search). Visitors are distinguished only by a daily-rotating hash, so no personal data is stored, and your own views are excluded
- Copy and share your referral link (luby.im/creators?ref=…) from the **Invite Friends, Get 500P** card on Home, and check signups, rewards, and this month's count. On campaign details, use **Share with a Friend** → copy a campaign-specific referral link (?ref). When a friend who signs up via your link **completes their first experience, you get 500P** (up to 5 per month). Check under "My Referral Signups" on the overview home

## 8. Notifications · Using It Like an App
- In-app notifications + email (toggle Progress Alerts / Reminders & Recommendations separately under `Settings → Email Notifications`)
- On a mobile browser, go to luby.im → **Add to Home Screen** → runs like an app (shortcuts to Campaigns, My Applications, Notifications)

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions
- **I'm not getting approved** → Check that your channel URL is a real profile and that your bio/specialties are filled in. If it's been over 24 hours, contact contact@plander.io
- **I don't see the Apply button** → You're either not yet approved, or the campaign's recruitment has closed
- **My points haven't arrived** → Points are credited after advertiser approval. Check the status (Submitted/Revision Requested/Approved) in My Applications

### Weekly Digest
Every **Monday at 9 AM**, you'll receive a single notification/email with: newly opened campaigns in your specialties/region, this week's deadlines, counts of pending applications/submissions needed/reviews pending, last week's approved and credited points, (if Public Profile is ON) last week's profile views and QR card traffic, and your withdrawal eligibility. You can turn this off under **Digest** in Settings > Email Notification Settings.

### Push Notifications
Tapping **Push Notifications > Turn on for this device** under Settings lets you receive selection, approval, and deadline alerts directly on your device even while the app is closed (the categories set in your email notification settings also apply to push). This works immediately on Android and PC Chrome; on **iPhone, you can enable it from the Luby AI app installed via Safari's Share → Add to Home Screen**. You can turn this on or off per device.

### My Portfolio (PDF)
Under Menu > **My Portfolio**, you can view your profile, channels, specialties, and collaboration history in a portfolio format to show to brands, and **save or print it as a PDF**. You can view it yourself even with Public Profile turned off; turning it on lets you share the same screen as a link (`luby.im/p/…`).
