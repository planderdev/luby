# Luby AI Creator Manual

> Target audience: Creators who apply to campaigns, complete experiences/publish content, and earn points · 최종 갱신 2026-08-19 · https://luby.im

> Pre-signup info page: **luby.im/creators** (EN: /en/creators, CN: /zh/creators) — See the number of campaigns currently recruiting, remaining spots, point levels, and how it works, all without logging in. Use this link when sharing with friends.

## 1. Sign Up and Approval
1. At https://luby.im/signup , select **Creator** (or continue with Google/Kakao → select Creator during onboarding)
2. Enter your name/nickname, activity region, primary channel (URL), and specialties (up to 3) → Sign Up → **Email Verification**
3. An admin reviews your channel/profile and **approves** it (usually within 24 hours). Once you receive an approval notification/email, you can apply to campaigns. You can browse campaigns even before approval.

## 2. Complete Your Profile — `Dashboard → Settings`
Bring the **Profile Completeness** gauge on the Overview home and top of Settings to 100%. A higher completeness rate leads to more recommendations and invitations.

| Item | Location | Tip |
|---|---|---|
| SNS Channels (30%) | Settings → Channels (`#channels`) | Selecting a channel type changes the URL/handle example. For **Xiaohongshu**, the `小红书号` in your app profile is the handle, and the URL is your profile share link (xhslink short links allowed) · Douyin uses `抖音号` · Lemon8 uses `lemon8-app.com/@…`. Addresses that don't match the selected channel type won't be saved |
| Specialties (25%) | Settings → Specialties (`#categories`) | Up to 3. Campaigns in your specialties are recommended first |
| Follower Count (15%) | Entered per channel | Used by advertiser filters and reach estimates |
| One-line Bio (15%) | Settings | The first thing advertisers read when selecting creators (10+ characters) |
| Activity Region (10%) · Profile Photo (5%) | Settings | Enables region-based recommendations · Profiles with a photo have higher selection rates |

## 3. Finding Campaigns — `Dashboard → Campaigns`

![Campaign directory — channel/sort filters and spots-left badges](/docs-img/directory.jpg)
- Default sort is **Recommended** (based on your specialties, region, and application status). Badges: `N spots left` (campaigns with fewer applicants than recruiting slots, giving higher selection odds), `My Specialty`, `My Region`, `Applied` / Sort by Newest, Deadline Soon, or Points, plus industry and region filters
- The public directory viewable without login (luby.im/c, including EN/CN) also supports **Deadline Soon, Newest, and Points** sorting and channel/region filters
- Overview home shows **"Campaigns for You"** (3 picks) · number of new campaigns this week · morning alerts when a **campaign in your specialty closes within 48 hours**
- **Instant New Campaign Alerts**: When a campaign matching your specialties (or your activity region + your channels) opens for recruitment, you'll get an immediate notification, push alert, and email (up to 1 per day, with the rest compiled into the Monday digest). The more complete your specialties, region, and channels are, the more accurate this becomes. You can turn this off under **Reminders & Recommendations** in `Settings → Email Notifications`.
- Campaign detail page: mission (per channel), what's provided, recruitment period, points, and advertiser profile link
- The **Selection Likelihood** card on the right of the campaign detail page shows competition ratio (applicants/recruiting slots, D-day), whether your specialty/region/channel matches, and your completion history, along with a High/Medium/Low rating and improvement tips (register a channel, add a specialty, application message). This is a reference estimate only — final selection is made by the advertiser

## 4. Applying · Invitations

![Public campaign page — reward, deadline, sign up & apply](/docs-img/campaign.jpg)
1. On the detail page, click **Apply** → write an application message (optional). Clicking **AI Draft** auto-fills 2–3 sentences based on the campaign mission and your profile (editable). If competition is high, differentiate with a concrete plan, focus on the channels you actually have, and never fabricate channels you haven't registered or experience you don't have. Completed experiences on Luby AI can be cited as credibility
2. You can **cancel your application** while it's pending. Duplicate applications to the same campaign aren't allowed
3. If an advertiser invites you, go to `Dashboard → Received Invitations` to **Accept (auto-applies)** or **Decline**
4. Once selected, you'll get a notification/email → coordinate schedules with the advertiser via **Chat** (`Dashboard → Messages`)

## 5. Experience → Content Submission → Review — `Dashboard → My Applications`
1. Publish content per the mission of your selected campaign (**disclosure of paid partnership, e.g. #ad #sponsored, is mandatory**)
2. **Submit Content**: publish URL + notes → sent to advertiser for review
3. **Approved** → points are credited immediately / **Revision Requested** → check feedback → **resubmit** on the same screen
4. If no submission is made after the experience period ends, you'll get a reminder

## 6. Points and Withdrawal — `Dashboard → Points`
- **Point Balance** (1P = 1 KRW) · **Point History**: content approvals (+campaign name), referral rewards, withdrawals, pending withdrawals, and rejections, shown chronologically
- **Withdrawal Request**: minimum 10,000P; enter bank, account number, and account holder name → points are deducted immediately upon request → paid out after admin verification (if rejected, automatically refunded with a reason shown)

## 7. Public Profile · Friend Referrals
- Toggle **ON** under `Settings → Public Profile` to get a shareable link at `luby.im/p/yourID` (KR/EN/CN) — shows only your name, photo, bio, region, specialties, channels, and completed collaborations (email and payment info stay private). Send this to advertisers or overseas brands as your portfolio
- From `Dashboard → My Portfolio`, save as PDF or **print a QR business card (A4)** — 10 standard business cards (90×55mm) print on a single sheet, and scanning the QR opens your public profile (requires Public Profile to be ON). Hand these out directly at store visits, events, or pop-ups
- When Public Profile is ON, the top of **My Portfolio** shows your profile view count (total, unique visits, last 7 days) and traffic sources (QR card / portfolio link / direct or search). Visitors are distinguished only by a daily-rotating hash — no personal data is stored, and your own views are excluded
- On the home screen, use the **Invite Friends & Earn 500P** card to copy/share your referral link (luby.im/creators?ref=…) and check signups, rewards, and this month's count. From a campaign detail page, use **Share with Friends** → copy a campaign-specific referral link (?ref). When a friend who signs up via your link **completes their first experience, you earn 500P** (up to 5 per month). Check this under "My Referral Signups" on the Overview home

## 8. Notifications · Using It Like an App
- In-app notifications + email (Progress Alerts / Reminders & Recommendations, each toggled independently in `Settings → Email Notifications`)
- On a mobile browser, go to luby.im → **Add to Home Screen** → launch it like an app (with shortcuts to Campaigns, My Applications, and Notifications)

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions
- **I'm not getting approved** → Check that your channel URL is a real profile and that your bio/specialties are filled in. If you've been waiting more than 24 hours, contact contact@plander.io
- **I don't see an Apply button** → You're either not yet approved, or the campaign's recruitment has closed
- **I haven't received my points** → Points are credited after advertiser approval. Check the status (Submitted/Revision Requested/Approved) in My Applications

### Weekly Digest
Every **Monday at 9 AM**, you'll receive a single notification/email covering: newly opened campaigns in your specialties/region, this week's deadlines, counts of pending applications/submissions needed/reviews pending, points approved and credited last week, (if Public Profile is ON) your profile views and QR card traffic from last week, and your withdrawal eligibility. You can turn this off under **Digest** in Settings > Email Notification Settings.

### Push Notifications
In Settings > **Push Notifications > Turn On for This Device**, you'll receive selection, approval, and deadline alerts directly on your device even while the app is closed (categories set in email notification settings also apply to push). Android and PC Chrome support this immediately; **on iPhone, you'll need to install the Luby AI app via Safari's Share → Add to Home Screen first**, then turn it on from there. Push can be turned on/off per device.

### My Portfolio (PDF)
From the menu > **My Portfolio**, you can view your profile, channels, specialties, and collaboration history in a portfolio format to show brands, and **save it as a PDF or print it**. You can view it yourself even with Public Profile turned off; turning it on lets you share the same view via a link (`luby.im/p/…`).
