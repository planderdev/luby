# Luby AI Creator Manual

> Target audience: Creators who apply to campaigns, complete experiences, publish content, and earn points · Last updated 2026-08-19 · https://luby.im

> Pre-signup info page: **luby.im/creators** (EN: /en/creators, CN: /zh/creators) — View the number of currently open campaigns, remaining spots, point levels, and how it works without logging in. Use this link when sharing with friends.

## 1. Sign Up and Approval

> **Language**: Sign-up, login, and password screens support Korean, English, and Chinese. Switch using **KR / EN / CN** in the top right of the screen — your choice is remembered, and if you arrive from an English or Chinese campaign page, it opens directly in that language. After logging in, the Home (overview, today's tasks, recommended campaigns), menu, campaign browsing (list, filters), campaign details, applications, and My Applications (content submission) all display in the same language, and English/Chinese users also see translated campaign titles and content. Settings (channels, specialties, public profile, notifications), received invitations, points/withdrawals, messages, and portfolio — the entire creator screen displays in the same language. In-app notifications, push notifications, and email notifications are also received in the same language (advertiser-written messages and invitation text remain in the original language).
1. At https://luby.im/signup, select **Creator** (or continue with Google/Kakao → select Creator during onboarding)
2. Enter your name/nickname, activity region, main channel (URL), and specialties (up to 3) → Sign Up → **Email Verification**
3. An admin reviews your channel/profile and **approves** it (usually within 24 hours). You can apply once you receive an approval notification/email. You can browse campaigns even before approval.

## 2. Complete Your Profile — `Dashboard → Settings`
Fill the **Profile Completion** gauge on the overview home and at the top of Settings to 100%. The higher your completion rate, the more recommendations and invitations you'll receive.

| Item | Location | Tip |
|---|---|---|
| SNS Channels (30%) | Settings → Channels (`#channels`) | The URL/handle examples change based on channel type. For **Xiaohongshu**, the handle is the `小红书号` from your app profile, and the URL is your profile share link (xhslink short links accepted) · Douyin uses `抖音号` · Lemon8 uses `lemon8-app.com/@…`. Addresses that don't match the channel type won't save |
| Specialties (25%) | Settings → Specialties (`#categories`) | Up to 3. Campaigns in your fields are recommended first |
| Follower Count (15%) | Entered per channel | Used by advertiser filters and estimated reach calculations |
| Short Bio (15%) | Settings | The first thing advertisers read when selecting creators (10+ characters) |
| Activity Region (10%) · Profile Photo (5%) | Settings | Used for region-based recommendations · Profiles with photos have higher selection rates |

## 3. Finding Campaigns — `Dashboard → Campaigns`

![Campaign directory — channel/sort filters and remaining-spots badge](/docs-img/directory.jpg)
- Default sort is **Recommended** (reflects your field, region, and application status). Badges: `N spots left` (campaigns with fewer applicants than recruitment capacity, offering higher selection odds), `My Field`, `My Region`, `Applied` / Sort by newest, closing soon, or points; filter by industry and region
- The public directory viewable without login (luby.im/c, including EN/CN) also supports sorting by **closing soon, newest, points** and channel/region filters
- Overview home shows 3 **"Campaigns for You"** · number of new campaigns this week · a morning alert if **a campaign in your field closes within 48 hours**
- **Instant new campaign alerts**: When a campaign matching your specialty (or your activity region + channels you have) opens for recruitment, you'll immediately receive a notification, push alert, and email (up to 1 per day, with the rest collected into the Monday digest). The more complete your specialty, region, and channel info, the more accurate these alerts become. You can turn this off under **Reminders & Recommendations** in `Settings → Email Notifications`.
- Campaign details: missions (by channel), what's provided, recruitment period, points, and advertiser profile link
- The **Selection Likelihood** card on the right of campaign details shows a High/Medium/Low rating along with improvement tips (register channels, add specialties, write an application message), based on competition ratio (applicants/recruitment capacity, days remaining), whether your field/region/channel match, and your completion history. This is a reference estimate only — final selection is made by the advertiser

## 4. Applying · Invitations

![Public campaign page — points, recruitment deadline, sign up and apply](/docs-img/campaign.jpg)
1. From the details page, click **Apply** → write an application message (optional). Clicking **AI Draft** fills in 2–3 sentences based on the campaign mission and your profile (editable). If competition is high, differentiate with a specific plan, focus on the channels you actually have, and never fabricate channels you haven't registered or experience you don't have. Completed experiences on Luby AI can be cited as credibility evidence
2. You can **Cancel Application** while it's pending. Duplicate applications to the same campaign aren't allowed
3. If an advertiser invites you, go to `Dashboard → Received Invitations` to **Accept (auto-applies)** / Decline
4. When selected, you'll get a notification/email → coordinate schedule with the advertiser via **Chat** (`Dashboard → Messages`)

## 5. Experience → Content Submission → Review — `Dashboard → My Applications`
1. Publish content according to the selected campaign's mission (**disclosure such as #ad #sponsored is required**)
2. **Submit Content**: Publication URL + notes → advertiser review
3. **Approved** → points credited immediately / **Revision Requested** → review feedback → **resubmit** on the same screen
4. If no submission is made after the experience period ends, you'll receive a reminder

## 6. Points and Withdrawals — `Dashboard → Points`
- **Point Balance** (1P = 1 KRW) · **Point History**: content approvals (+ campaign name), referral rewards, withdrawals, pending withdrawals, rejections — shown in chronological order
- **Withdrawal Request**: Minimum 10,000P, enter bank/account/account holder name → deducted immediately upon request → paid after admin verification (if rejected, automatically refunded with reason shown)

## 7. Public Profile · Friend Referrals
- Toggle `Settings → Public Profile` ON → get a `luby.im/p/yourID` link (KR/EN/CN) — shows only name, photo, bio, region, field, channels, and completed collaborations (email and payment info stay private). Send it to advertisers and overseas brands as a portfolio
- From `Dashboard → My Portfolio`, save as PDF or **print a QR business card (A4)** → 10 business cards (90×55mm) print per sheet; scanning the QR opens your public profile (requires Public Profile to be ON). Hand it directly to shop owners during store visits, events, or pop-ups
- When your public profile is on, **My Portfolio** shows profile view stats (total, unique visits, last 7 days) and traffic sources (QR card / portfolio link / direct or search) at the top. Visitors are distinguished only by a daily-rotating hash with no personal data stored, and your own views are excluded
- From the home **Invite Friends, Get 500P** card, copy/share your referral link (luby.im/creators?ref=…) and check sign-ups, rewards, and this month's count. On campaign details, use **Share with Friends** → copy a campaign-specific referral link (?ref). When a friend who joins via your link **completes their first experience, you get 500P** (up to 5 per month). Check under "My Referral Sign-ups" on the overview home

## 8. Notifications · Using It Like an App
- In-app notifications + email (toggle Progress Updates / Reminders & Recommendations independently in `Settings → Email Notifications`)
- On mobile browsers, go to luby.im → **Add to Home Screen** → run it like an app (shortcuts: Campaigns, My Applications, Notifications)

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions
- **My approval isn't coming through** → Check that your channel URL is a real profile and that your bio/field are filled in. If it's been pending over 24 hours, contact contact@plander.io
- **I don't see an Apply button** → You're either not yet approved, or the campaign's recruitment period has closed
- **My points haven't arrived** → Points are credited after advertiser approval. Check the status (Submitted/Revision Requested/Approved) in My Applications

### Weekly Digest
Every **Monday at 9 AM**, you'll receive a single notification/email covering: newly opened campaigns in your field/region, campaigns closing this week, counts of applications pending, content needing submission, and reviews pending, points approved/credited last week, (if Public Profile is ON) last week's profile views and QR card traffic, and your withdrawal eligibility. You can turn this off under **Digest** in Settings > Email Notification Settings.

### Push Notifications
Tap **Push Notifications > Turn On for This Device** in Settings to receive selection, approval, and deadline alerts directly on your device even when the app is closed (the categories in your email notification settings also apply to push). Works immediately on Android and PC Chrome; on **iPhone, enable it from the Luby AI app installed via Safari's Share → Add to Home Screen**. Can be turned on/off per device.

### My Portfolio (PDF)
Under Menu > **My Portfolio**, you can view your profile, channels, specialties, and collaboration history in a portfolio format to show brands, and **save as PDF or print**. You can view it yourself even with Public Profile off; turning it on lets you share the same view via a link (`luby.im/p/…`).
