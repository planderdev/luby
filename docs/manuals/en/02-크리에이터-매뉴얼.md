# Luby AI Creator Manual

> Audience: Creators who apply to campaigns, complete experiences, publish content, and earn points · Last updated 2026-08-19 · https://luby.im

> Pre-signup info page: **luby.im/creators** (EN: /en/creators, CN: /zh/creators) — See the number of campaigns currently recruiting, remaining slots, point levels, and how it works without logging in. Use this link when sharing with friends.

## 1. Sign Up and Approval

> **Language**: The signup, login, and password screens support Korean, English, and Chinese. Switching **KR / EN / CN** at the top right of the screen remembers your choice, and if you arrive from an English or Chinese campaign page, it opens directly in that language. After logging in, the Home (overview, today's tasks, recommended campaigns), Menu, Browse Campaigns (list, filters), Campaign Details, Apply, and My Applications (content submission) all display in the same language, and English/Chinese users also see campaign titles and content in translation. The entire creator experience — Settings (channels, specialties, public profile, notifications), Received Invitations, Points & Withdrawal, Messages, and Portfolio — displays in the same language. In-app notifications, push notifications, and email alerts are also delivered in the same language (messages and invitations written directly by advertisers remain in their original language).
1. Go to https://luby.im/signup and select **Creator** (or continue with Google/Kakao → select Creator during onboarding)
2. Enter your name/nickname, activity region, main channel (URL), and specialties (up to 3) → Sign Up → **Verify Email**
3. An admin reviews your channel and profile, then **approves** it (usually within 24 hours). Once you receive the approval notification/email, you can apply. You can browse campaigns even before approval.

## 2. Complete Your Profile — `Dashboard → Settings`
Fill the **Profile Completion** gauge at the top of the overview home and Settings page to 100%. A higher completion rate leads to more recommendations and invitations.

| Item | Location | Tip |
|---|---|---|
| SNS Channel (30%) | Settings → Channels (`#channels`) | Selecting a channel type changes the URL/handle example. For **Xiaohongshu**, the handle is the `小红书号` in your app profile, and the URL is your profile share link (xhslink short links allowed) · Douyin uses `抖音号` · Lemon8 uses `lemon8-app.com/@…`. Addresses that don't match the channel type won't be saved |
| Specialties (25%) | Settings → Specialties (`#categories`) | Up to 3. Campaigns matching your specialties are recommended first |
| Follower Count (15%) | Entered per channel | Used by advertiser filters and estimated reach calculations |
| One-line Bio (15%) | Settings | The first thing advertisers read when selecting creators (10+ characters) |
| Activity Region (10%) · Profile Photo (5%) | Settings | Used for region-based recommendations · Profiles with photos have higher selection rates |

## 3. Find Campaigns — `Dashboard → Campaigns`

![Campaign directory — channel/sort filters and slots-remaining badge](/docs-img/directory.jpg)
- Default sort is **Recommended** (reflects your specialties, region, and application status). Badges: `N slots remaining` (campaigns with fewer applicants than recruitment capacity, meaning higher selection odds), `My Specialty`, `My Region`, `Applied` / Sort by newest, deadline soon, or points, plus industry and region filters
- The public directory viewable without login (luby.im/c, including EN/CN) also supports **deadline soon, newest, and points** sorting and channel/region filters
- Overview home shows 3 **"Campaigns For You"** · number of new campaigns this week · a morning alert if **a campaign in your specialty is closing within 48 hours**
- **Instant new campaign alerts**: When a campaign matching your specialties (or your activity region + the channels you have) starts recruiting, you'll get an immediate notification, push, and email (max 1 per day; the rest are compiled into the Monday digest). The more complete your specialties, region, and channels are, the more accurate this gets. You can turn this off under **Reminders & Recommendations** in `Settings → Email Notifications`.
- Campaign Details: Missions (per channel), what's provided, recruitment period, points, and a link to the advertiser's profile
- The **Selection Likelihood** card on the right of Campaign Details shows a High/Medium/Low rating based on competition ratio (applicants/recruitment capacity, days remaining), match with your specialty/region/channel, and your completion history, along with improvement tips (register a channel, add a specialty, write an application message). This is an estimate for reference only — final selection is made by the advertiser

## 4. Apply · Invitations

![Public campaign page — points, recruitment deadline, sign up and apply](/docs-img/campaign.jpg)
1. On the details page, click **Apply** → write an application message (optional). Pressing **AI Draft** fills in 2–3 sentences based on the campaign mission and your profile (editable). If competition is high, differentiate with a specific plan, focus on the channels you actually have, and never fabricate channels you haven't registered or experience you don't have. Completed experiences on Luby AI can be cited as proof of credibility
2. While your application is pending, you can **Cancel Application**. Duplicate applications to the same campaign aren't allowed
3. If invited by an advertiser, go to `Dashboard → Received Invitations` to **Accept (auto-apply)** or **Decline**
4. Once selected, you'll get a notification/email → coordinate schedule with the advertiser via **Chat** (`Dashboard → Messages`)

## 5. Experience → Submit Content → Review — `Dashboard → My Applications`
1. Publish content according to the selected campaign's mission (**disclosure of sponsorship, e.g. #ad #sponsored, is required**)
2. **Submit Content**: Enter the published URL + notes → sent for advertiser review
3. **Approved** → points credited immediately / **Revision Requested** → check feedback → **resubmit** on the same screen
4. If no submission is made after the experience period ends, a reminder will be sent

## 6. Points and Withdrawal — `Dashboard → Points`
- **Point Balance** (1P = 1 KRW) · **Point History**: Content approvals (+campaign name), referral rewards, withdrawals, pending withdrawals, and rejections, shown chronologically
- **Request Withdrawal**: Minimum 10,000P. Enter bank, account number, and account holder name → points are deducted immediately upon request → paid out after admin confirmation (automatically refunded with a reason shown if rejected)
- **Withdrawal Account**: Currently, withdrawals are only supported to **Korean bank accounts** (international accounts not supported). For overseas creators, the provided experience/product itself is the main reward; points can be withdrawn once you have a Korean bank account.

## 7. Public Profile · Friend Referral
- Toggle `Settings → Public Profile` ON → get a `luby.im/p/yourID` link (KR/EN/CN) — shows only your name, photo, bio, region, specialties, channels, and completed collaborations (email/contact/payout info stays private). Share it with advertisers or overseas brands as your portfolio
- From `Dashboard → My Portfolio`, save as a PDF or **print a QR business card on A4** → 10 business-card-sized (90×55mm) cards fit on one sheet, and scanning the QR opens your public profile (Public Profile must be ON). Hand it directly to business owners during store visits, events, or pop-ups
- When Public Profile is on, the top of **My Portfolio** shows profile view counts (total, unique visitors, last 7 days) and traffic sources (QR business card / portfolio link / direct or search). Visitors are distinguished only by a daily-rotating hash, so no personal data is stored, and your own views are excluded
- Copy and share your referral link (luby.im/creators?ref=…) from the **Invite Friends & Get 500P** card on the home page, and check signups, rewards, and this month's count. On Campaign Details, use **Share with Friends** → copy a campaign-specific referral link (?ref). When a friend signs up via your link and **completes their first experience, you get 500P** (up to 5 per month). Check this under "My Referral Signups" on the overview home

## 8. Notifications · Using It Like an App
- In-app notifications + email (Progress Alerts / Reminders & Recommendations, each toggleable under `Settings → Email Notifications`)
- On mobile browser, go to luby.im → **Add to Home Screen** → runs like an app (shortcuts: Campaigns, My Applications, Notifications)

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions
- **I'm not getting approved** → Check that your channel URL is an actual profile and that your bio and specialties are filled in. If you've waited more than 24 hours, contact contact@plander.io
- **I don't see the Apply button** → You're not yet approved, or recruitment for the campaign has closed
- **I haven't received my points** → Points are credited after advertiser approval. Check the status (Submitted/Revision Requested/Approved) in My Applications

### Weekly Digest
Every **Monday at 9 AM**, you'll receive a single notification/email summarizing new campaigns in your specialties/region, this week's deadlines, counts of pending applications/submissions needed/under review, points approved and credited last week, (if Public Profile is ON) last week's profile views and QR business card traffic, and your withdrawal eligibility. You can turn this off under **Digest** in Settings > Email Notification Settings.

### Push Notifications
Tapping **Push Notifications > Turn on for this device** in Settings lets selection, approval, and deadline alerts reach your device immediately even while the app is closed (the categories in your email notification settings apply to push as well). Android and PC Chrome can enable this directly, while **on iPhone, you can turn it on from the Luby AI app installed via Safari Share → Add to Home Screen**. Can be turned on/off per device.

### My Portfolio (PDF)
From Menu > **My Portfolio**, view your profile, channels, specialties, and collaboration history in a portfolio format to show brands, and **save as PDF or print**. You can view this yourself even with Public Profile off, and when it's on, you can share the same page via a link (`luby.im/p/…`).
